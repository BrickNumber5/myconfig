## Logos

This repository contains a number of icons/logos belonging to other people, for
instance in the home/.config/firefox-custom/skeleton-profile/search.json.sidecar
directory. These icons are the property of their respective owners. I believe
my inclusion of these icons constitutes fair use as they are used to identify,
accurately, the groups and products to which they refer. As an example, the icon
home/.config/firefox-custom/skeleton-profile/seach.json.sidecar/mdn-16.ico is
the mdn logo and is included into search.json.mozlz4 to provide an mdn logo in
the search bar when searching using the mdn "search engine" in firefox.
